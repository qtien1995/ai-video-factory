# Risk Term Groups

Use this as a review list. The safer move is usually to remove, neutralize, or contextualize risky wording, not to disguise harmful content.

## Health and Body

Disease names, body parts, medical treatment, "cure", "guarantee", graphic bodily functions, disability, epidemic, dramatic symptoms.

## Finance

Loan, debt, credit, interest, tax, disbursement, financial status, rich/poor claims, investment promises.

## Violence and Crime

Kill, stab, beat, cut, blood, weapon, drugs, kidnapping, extortion, assault, murder, theft, fraud, corruption, revenge, war, gore, dead body.

## Sexual Content and Minors

Sexual exploitation, coercion, nudity, sexual acts, solicitation, minors, private body parts, explicit sounds/poses.

## Hate and Harassment

Slurs, dehumanizing comparisons, insults about appearance, hygiene, intelligence, disability, morality, religion, race, gender, sexuality, or nationality.

## Controlled Goods

Tobacco, alcohol, weapons, explosives, drugs, non-medical pharmaceuticals, adult services.

## Copyright and Brand

Famous brands, logos, reuploaded celebrity images, unlicensed clips, screenshots, music, and private messages.
