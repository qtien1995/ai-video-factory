# Audience Avatar

Build the customer avatar from real behavior, not just demographics.

## Required Fields

- Demographics: age, gender, location, job, income range, family/life stage.
- Goals: what they are trying to achieve.
- Challenges: what blocks them.
- Pain points: before, during, after the problem appears.
- Values: what they respect, admire, or avoid.
- Negative information: what they dislike, fear, or do not want to be associated with.
- Content behavior: where they scroll, what they save, who they trust, what comments they write.
- Buying barrier: why they do not buy yet.
- Trust trigger: what proof would make them believe.

## Ways to Research

- Analyze competitor ads, landing pages, hooks, comments, and frequently repeated claims.
- Read groups, reviews, Q&A, comments, and customer messages.
- Use available customer data and sales conversations.
- Ask AI to generate hypotheses, then verify with real data.
