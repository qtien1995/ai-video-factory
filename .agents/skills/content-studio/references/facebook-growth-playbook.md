# Facebook Growth Playbook

## Course-Derived 8-Step Logic

1. Understand Facebook: feed ranking, inventory, prediction, value scoring, reduced distribution, community standards, copyright.
2. Define the page objective.
3. Analyze competitors and related pages.
4. Identify target audience and content topics.
5. Build content lines for awareness, trust, conversion, and care.
6. Produce consistent posts and collect data.
7. Interact and seed early distribution ethically.
8. Measure, repeat winners, and expand.

## Distribution Heuristics

Facebook considers the available content inventory, signals about the viewer/page relationship, predictions about engagement, and value/relevance scoring.

Practical implications:

- Connection matters. Comments, replies, profile/page visits, and repeated reading/viewing strengthen future distribution.
- Dwell time matters for posts. A long post only works if people actually read it.
- For video/Reels, first seconds, watch time, completion, shares, and comments matter.
- Risky comments under a post can hurt distribution even if the post itself is not obviously violating.

## Page Stages

### New / learning stage

Goal: help the platform learn the page and audience.

- Keep the topic cluster tight.
- Post consistently for 3-4 weeks before judging.
- Use warm network or relevant cross-share carefully when available.
- Do not jump between unrelated concepts every day.

### First viral stage

Goal: convert reach into audience identity.

- Turn the winning topic into a series.
- Add trust posts after reach spikes.
- Invite discussion and profile visits.

### Growth stage

Goal: build stable repeatable formats.

- Balance reach content with trust and conversion.
- Refresh angles before audience fatigue.
- Watch follower growth, comment quality, and direct messages, not only reach.

### Conversion stage

Goal: make business result visible.

- Add proof, objection handling, customer stories, offers, and clear CTA.
- Avoid suddenly switching to sales-only posting.

## Content Mix

- 40% reach/value/relatable
- 25% trust/proof/case/process
- 20% community/Q&A/opinion
- 15% conversion/offer/lead magnet

Adjust by stage and business model.
