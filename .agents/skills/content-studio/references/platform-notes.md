# Platform Notes

## Facebook Reels

- Entertainment, clarity, and empathy are the core style.
- Brand/product should be visible but not disruptive.
- CTA is useful for ads and conversion content.
- Repurpose existing assets by changing first frame, pacing, text overlay, and story order.

## TikTok

- First 3 seconds, completion, rewatch, and topic-label clarity matter strongly.
- A video should create a reason to follow, not only a reason to watch.
- Series and repeatable formats are useful after a winning concept appears.

## Universal Short-Video Checks

- Is the hook concrete?
- Is the viewer promise obvious?
- Is the video one idea, not three?
- Does each shot earn its place?
- Does the ending give payoff or next action?
