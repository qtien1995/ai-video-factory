## 6. Success/Failure Diagnosis

Strong reasons:

- clear audience
- platform timing
- repeatable concept
- strong first seconds
- credible persona
- traffic seed
- content-market fit

Failure reasons:

- broad topic with weak conversion
- wrong format for resources
- inconsistent concept
- no proof/trust
- over-reliance on trend
- policy/copyright risk
- no measurement loop
