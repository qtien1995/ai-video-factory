# Channel Plan Template

## 1. 5C Analysis

| Area | Questions |
|---|---|
| Context | What is happening in the market, platform, category, culture, and timing? |
| Customer | Who are the key segments? What do they want, fear, misunderstand, and already watch? |
| Competitor | Who is already getting attention? What formats/topics work? Where are their gaps? |
| Collaborator | Who can lend traffic, proof, distribution, expertise, or credibility? |
| Company/Self | What strengths, weaknesses, proof, resources, and risks already exist? |

## 2. STP

- Segmentation: split the market into specific behavior and need groups.
- Targeting: choose a segment that is valuable, reachable, and not too broad.
- Positioning: choose one keyword or promise to plant in the audience's mind.

Examples of positioning keywords from course logic:

- tận tâm
- dễ hiểu
- chân thành
- thực chiến
- chuyên sâu
- truyền cảm hứng

## 3. Content Funnel

| Funnel | Job | Content examples |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness/Like | Make people know and enjoy the channel | viral lessons, relatable stories, useful tips, hot-topic angle |
| Trust | Make people believe the person/brand | case study, proof, behind the scenes, process, feedback, expert view |
| Conversion | Make the next action clear | offer post, objection handling, DM CTA, lead magnet, event/workshop |
| Care/Fan | Make people stay and advocate | Q&A, community post, customer spotlight, advanced help |

## 4. 5W1H Planning Sheet

| Item | Fill in |
|---|---|
| Goal | What business or channel result does this post support? |
| Pain/Desire | What viewer tension does it touch? |
| Audience | Who is the post for? |
| Format | Status, Reels, TikTok, carousel, image, live, long post |
| Hook/title | First sentence or first 3 seconds |
| Structure | Opening, body beats, CTA |
| Proof | Data, case, screenshot, process, credential, story |
| Metric | Reach, saves, comments, followers, leads, orders |

## 5. 30-Day Test

- Week 1: prove audience pain and hook styles.
- Week 2: repeat the best 2-3 formats with new angles.
- Week 3: add trust and proof content.
- Week 4: test conversion content and review metrics.

Keep enough consistency to read signals. Do not change niche, format, and audience all at once.
