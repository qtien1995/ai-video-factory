# Facebook Risk Checklist

## Protected Groups and Identity

Be careful with content about:

- minors under 18
- skin color, ethnicity, nationality
- gender, sexual orientation
- religion
- disability, mental health, illness
- immigration/refugee status

Do not attack, shame, mock, dehumanize, or expose private information.

## High-Risk Topics

### Sexual abuse or minors

- Do not show victim face, name, age, address, phone, ID, or family details.
- Avoid describing the act.
- Prefer text/status with careful sourcing over graphic images.
- Protect the identity of minors, victims, and related people.

### Crime, accidents, public scandals

- Blur or avoid faces of victims, suspects, minors, and bystanders.
- Blur weapons, blood, drugs, graphic injuries, license plates, and private locations.
- Use credible news sources and avoid sensational wording.

### Self-harm and suicide

- Do not show scenes, methods, bodies, blood, or step-by-step details.
- Do not joke, encourage, or romanticize self-harm.
- Use neutral, compassionate framing.

### Celebrities and public allegations

- Positive public content is lower risk.
- For allegations or private-life content, avoid direct accusation unless verified by reputable sources.
- Consider using illustrative images rather than faces.

### Private-message posts

- Remove Messenger UI, names, avatars, phone numbers, addresses, account handles, and third-party names.
- Remove insults, sexual wording, threats, and harassment.
- Avoid posting screenshots if the same point can be summarized.

### Brand criticism

- Use facts, dates, receipts, and source links.
- Avoid logo-heavy images when not necessary.
- Avoid defamatory claims without evidence.
- Prefer "my experience" or "according to source" over sweeping accusations.

## Final Checklist

- Sensitive identifiers removed.
- Graphic details reduced or removed.
- Images blurred/cropped where needed.
- Credible source added for news/claims.
- Spelling checked.
- Hashtags are relevant and not inflammatory.
- CTA does not encourage harassment, dogpiling, or illegal action.
