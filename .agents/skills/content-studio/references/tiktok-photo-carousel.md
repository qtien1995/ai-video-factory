# TikTok Photo Carousel

Use photo carousel when the idea benefits from sequential reading, visual proof, lists, step-by-step logic, emotional storytelling, or before/after contrast.

## Carousel Structure

1. Slide 1: hook/title. Make the reason to swipe obvious.
2. Slides 2-4: build curiosity or useful value.
3. Middle slides: proof, details, steps, examples, or emotional turning point.
4. Final slide: summary, lesson, CTA, or question.

## Good Uses

- "7 dấu hiệu..."
- before/after transformation
- mini case study
- personal journey
- checklists
- mistake breakdown
- templates
- screenshots with explanation

## Production Rules

- One idea per slide.
- Text must be readable on mobile.
- Keep visual style consistent.
- Use strong first slide and a reason to continue.
- Avoid turning every video idea into carousel; choose it when reading/swiping increases retention.
- If a carousel is used to sell, build trust before CTA.
