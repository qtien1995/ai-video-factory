# TikTok Growth Notes

## Distribution Heuristics

Course notes describe TikTok as heavily behavior-driven and AI-personalized.

Practical implications:

- The platform tests content with a small initial group and expands when signals are good.
- Watch completion and rewatch are especially important signals.
- Likes/comments matter, but they do not rescue a video that people do not watch.
- A channel can be associated with content labels. Too many unrelated concepts can confuse distribution.
- Short videos can perform well when the completion and rewatch rates are strong.

## Niche Formula

Strong channel concept = platform direction + audience interest + creator/business resource + differentiated angle.

Ask:

- What is the platform currently willing to distribute?
- What does the target audience already watch?
- What can the creator produce consistently?
- What is the special angle that is not already everywhere?

## Growth Stages

### Stage 1: Find concept

- Test audience, hook, and format.
- Keep tests controlled. Do not change everything at once.

### Stage 2: Repeat winner

- Keep the winning concept.
- Turn it into a series.
- Improve first 3 seconds and structure.

### Stage 3: Expand topics

- Keep the channel promise stable.
- Add adjacent topics that still serve the same audience.

### Stage 4: Build trust/conversion

- Add proof, experience, behind-the-scenes, customer stories, offer, and CTA.
- Track follower conversion, profile visits, DMs, link clicks, and sales.

## Metrics

- Views/reach
- Average watch time
- Completion rate
- Rewatch behavior
- Shares
- Saves
- Comment quality
- Follower growth per video
- Profile visits
- Orders/leads/DMs when selling
