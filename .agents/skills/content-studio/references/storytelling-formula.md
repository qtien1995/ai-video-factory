# Storytelling Formula

## 1. Mở bài

Use one short, easy sentence that goes straight into the problem and emotion. The opening should create xúc động, đồng cảm, hoặc tò mò.

Formula:

- [Character] + [emotional action] + [unexpected problem]
- [Time marker] + [event] + [why it matters]

Example shapes:

- "Minh vừa khóc vừa gấp 1000 con hạc giấy để cầu mong em trai sống lại."
- "10 giờ sáng hôm đó, bố tôi gọi điện về bằng một giọng bình tĩnh đến lạ."

## 2. Thân bài

A story becomes more engaging when it has a clear climax. Build toward it instead of explaining everything at once.

Include:

- Where: setting, room, street, school, hospital, shop, office.
- Who: main character, supporting character, relationship.
- When: date, age, season, time of day, before/after marker.
- What changed: the event that makes this story worth telling.
- Dialogue: one or two lines that reveal fear, love, conflict, or realization.
- Inner voice: what the character could not say out loud.

## 3. Chi tiết hóa

Replace abstract emotion with evidence:

- Instead of "tôi rất sợ", show the trembling hand, missed calls, repeated checking, or silence in the room.
- Instead of "mẹ thương tôi", show the specific object, sacrifice, small routine, or sentence she repeated.
- Instead of "tôi thất bại", show the bill, debt message, empty inbox, failed pitch, or lonely ride home.

Use enough detail to make the reader see the scene, but remove details that do not move the emotion forward.

## 4. Kết bài

End with a personal viewpoint, lesson, or emotional realization after the event.

Strong endings:

- reveal a changed belief
- name the hidden love behind an ordinary action
- connect a small detail to a bigger lesson
- leave one sentence that the reader can carry away

Avoid:

- preaching too long
- adding a sales pitch that breaks the story
- forcing tears with exaggeration

## Story Types From Course Material

- Hành trình: show a hard-to-achieve transformation through time markers.
- Cảm động: focus on separation, sacrifice, illness, poverty, reunion, gratitude, or human kindness with respectful distance.
- Liên kết nguyên nhân - hậu quả: make the cause and result clear, logical, and emotionally meaningful.
- Tốt đẹp bất ngờ: start with a negative or ordinary situation and reveal a positive ending.
- Before/after: make the contrast visible and specific.
