# Case Examples From Module 4

## Hyystudies

Useful lesson: a page can grow from a clear long-term value even before heavy monetization. The repeated values were inspiration, positive energy, and school/youth-related emotion. The creator had self-driven resources: time, writing habit, and frequent short sharing.

## Thái Hà Tuyển Dụng

Useful lesson: expert channels can work when the topic expands from a specific audience into wider social concerns. The course emphasizes matching the target audience's concern with broader community concern, then using repeatable content and early distribution support.

## Home English

Useful lesson: timing and resources matter. The case started in an early TikTok education period, had subject-matter experts/teachers as faces, multiple writers, and editing support. The initial goal was experimentation and trend capture, not immediate conversion.

## Diệu Hoa LangGo

Useful lesson: personal-brand positioning needs a human voice, not only expertise. The course points to inspirational/giving positioning and a warmer delivery style: advise like a friend, tell a story, then draw a lesson.

## Leo Minh Marketing

Useful lesson: camera angle and delivery affect authority and distance. Looking straight into camera can work for direct expertise, but the format must match the creator's natural strength and audience expectation.

## Success/Failure Session

The course analyzes both successful and failed channels by starting with resources, objective, audience, topic, platform timing, distribution, and what the creator repeated after a first signal. A successful page may begin with limited resources if the concept, timing, and traffic seed are strong; failure often comes from changing too many concepts before the platform or audience can learn.
